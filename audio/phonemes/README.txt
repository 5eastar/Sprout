UK English Phoneme Audio Files
==============================

a.ogg, b.ogg, c.ogg, d.ogg, e.ogg, f.ogg, g.ogg, h.ogg, i.ogg, j.ogg,
k.ogg, l.ogg, m.ogg, n.ogg, o.ogg, p.ogg, q.ogg, r.ogg, s.ogg, t.ogg,
u.ogg, v.ogg, w.ogg, x.ogg, y.ogg, z.ogg

Digraphs: ch.ogg, sh.ogg, th.ogg, ng.ogg, ck.ogg, qu.ogg

WHERE TO GET PHONICS AUDIO:
---------------------------
Option 1: Record yourself (recommended for UK phonics accuracy)
- Use phone voice recorder or Audacity
- Record each sound in isolation as taught in Letters and Sounds
- Keep files short (under 1 second each)

Option 2: Screen record from phonics websites
- Phonics Play, Letters and Sounds videos, etc.
- Extract the audio for each phoneme

Option 3: Ask your school
- Many schools have phonics audio resources
- Check with your phonics lead or SENCO

The app will use speech synthesis as a fallback if no audio files are present.
